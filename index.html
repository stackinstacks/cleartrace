Methodology

1. Resolve identity
Compare name, jurisdiction and other non-sensitive identifiers.

2. Retrieve sources
Prefer courts, government registries, official organizations and reputable research.

3. Corroborate
Separate matching records from similarly named people.

4. Classify
Confirmed, likely match, possible match, contradictory, or unresolved.

5. Report
Show source, date, confidence and limitations for every material finding.

Important safeguards

• Minimize personal data: Avoid displaying home addresses, exact birth dates, family/associate lists, phone numbers, emails, or other sensitive details unless strictly necessary and lawful.
• No sensitive inference: Never infer race, religion, health, political affiliation, sexual orientation, or similar traits.
• Avoid presumption of guilt: Do not equate an arrest, allegation, lawsuit, or name match with guilt.
• Preserve provenance: Allow a reviewer to inspect the underlying record and retrieval timestamp.
• Provide redress: Include correction/dispute and record-status workflows where appropriate.
• Apply controls: Rate limits, access controls, audit logs, retention limits, and encryption.
• Regulatory compliance: For employment or regulated background screening, use a compliant provider with required legal notices.

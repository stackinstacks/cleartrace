Methodology

1. Resolve identity
Compare name, jurisdiction and other non-sensitive identifiers.

2. Retrieve sources
Prefer courts, government registries, official organizations and reputable reporting.

3. Corroborate
Separate matching records from similarly named people.

4. Classify
Confirmed, likely match, possible match, contradictory, or unresolved.

5. Report
Show source, date, confidence and limitations for every material finding.

Important safeguards for a production deployment

• Minimize personal data: avoid displaying home addresses, exact birth dates, family/associate lists, phone numbers, emails, or other sensitive details unless strictly necessary and lawful.
• Never infer race, religion, health, political affiliation, sexual orientation, or similar sensitive traits.
• Do not equate an arrest, allegation, lawsuit, or name match with guilt.
• Preserve source provenance and retrieval timestamps; allow a reviewer to inspect the underlying record.
• Provide correction/dispute and record-status workflows where appropriate.
• Apply rate limits, access controls, audit logs, retention limits, and encryption.
• For employment or other regulated background-screening use, use a compliant screening provider and required legal notices/consent processes instead of treating this tool as a consumer-reporting agency.
